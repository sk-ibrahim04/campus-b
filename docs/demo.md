# CAMPUSSYNAPSE — 2-MINUTE JUDGE DEMONSTRATION SCRIPT

**Smart India Hackathon 2026 — Problem Statement SIH26193**

---

## The 60-Second Hook
> *"Judges, traditional college ERPs are dumb forms: a teacher submits a booking, an admin checks an Excel sheet, and clashes happen every single day. CampusSynapse is the complete opposite: it is an autonomous Mission Control for a smart campus that senses the live digital twin, predicts clashes, uses constraint optimization to generate explainable candidate plans, and safely orchestrates resources."*

---

## 2-Minute Step-by-Step Flow

### STEP 1: Show the Live Digital Twin (0:00 – 0:30)
1. Open `http://localhost:5173/digital-twin`.
2. Point to the 3 blocks:
   - **Block A**: Engineering & heavy labs.
   - **Block B**: Central academic complex.
   - **Block C**: Advanced Computing & Auditorium.
3. Click on **Auditorium 1** or **Classroom B201**:
   - Show the slide-out drawer with **live IoT telemetry**: live occupancy count, ambient temperature, power draw (Watts), and sensor heartbeat.
   - Note the status colors: Green for Available, Cyan for Occupied, Amber for Maintenance.

### STEP 2: The Hero Command & Orchestration (0:30 – 1:15)
1. Go to **Dashboard** or click **RUN DEMO**.
2. Show the prominent AI Command Bar:
   > *"Plan a seminar for 180 students tomorrow from 2 PM to 5 PM."*
3. Click **ORCHESTRATE**:
   - Watch the live 7-stage execution pipeline complete in real time.
   - Show that **Seminar Hall A was flagged for a timetable conflict** (Guest lecture at 2:30 PM).
   - Show the **3 candidate plans generated by Google OR-Tools CP-SAT**:
     - **Plan A (Recommended)**: Seminar Hall B (190 seats, 98% utilization, 0 conflict).
     - **Plan B**: Auditorium 1 (350 seats, lower utilization penalty).
     - **Plan C**: Block C Multi-purpose Room.
4. Show the **Decision Card ("Why this decision?")**:
   - Point to the checklist: Capacity certified, zero timetable clash, projector verified, elevator access verified.
   - Point to the multi-attribute score breakdown: Feasibility (35%), Min Disruption (25%), Capacity Fit (20%), Proximity (10%).

### STEP 3: Human-in-the-Loop Sign-Off & Live Update (1:15 – 1:45)
1. Click **AUTHORIZE & EXECUTE PLAN**.
2. Notice the instant toast notification.
3. Open `/digital-twin`:
   - Watch **Seminar Hall B switch from AVAILABLE to RESERVED** live over Socket.IO without any page reload!
4. Open `/audit-log`:
   - Show the immutable audit trail recording the exact timestamp, actor, agent, previous state, and rationale.

### STEP 4: What-If Simulation & Safety Resilience (1:45 – 2:15)
1. Navigate to `/simulator`.
2. Select *"Block C Power Grid Outage"* or *"Auditorium 1 Sudden Closure"*.
3. Click **EXECUTE WHAT-IF SIMULATION**:
   - Show that the system predicted 6 affected classes and 240 displaced students, and proposed automated rerouting.
   - **Crucial judge point**: Emphasize that the real campus database was **NOT mutated** (0 state corruption guarantee).

### STEP 5: Autonomous Resource Recovery (2:15 – 2:30)
1. Point to the Purple Banner:
   - *"Ghost booking detected in Classroom B204: 45 seats reserved, but IoT occupancy reports 0 people."*
2. Click **RECLAIM GHOST RESERVATION**:
   - Room is instantly restored to AVAILABLE and made open to the waitlist.
3. Conclude:
   > *"Observe. Simulate. Decide. Automate. That is CampusSynapse."*
